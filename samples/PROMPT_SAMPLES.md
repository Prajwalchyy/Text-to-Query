## 5. CSV Spreadsheets (.csv — Pandas Mode)

*Note: In CSV mode, OmniData AI generates and executes idiomatic Python / Pandas code in a secure AST-guarded sandbox.*

### 5.1 `samples/5_csv_spreadsheets/single/sales_performance.csv`
> **Columns:** `OrderID`, `CustomerName`, `Region`, `Rep`, `Product`, `Units`, `UnitPrice`, `TotalRevenue`, `PaymentMethod`  
> **Upload Mode:** Single Upload (saves into `data/csv/single/`)

| # | Difficulty | Test Prompt to Copy & Paste | Expected Pandas Code Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Beginner | `Show all sales transactions where total revenue is greater than $2,000.` | `df[df['TotalRevenue'] > 2000]` |
| **2** | Intermediate | `Calculate the total revenue generated by each sales rep.` | `df.groupby('Rep')['TotalRevenue'].sum().reset_index()` |
| **3** | Intermediate | `Filter for transactions in the 'East' or 'West' regions with more than 10 units sold.` | `df[(df['Region'].isin(['East', 'West'])) & (df['Units'] > 10)]` |
| **4** | Advanced | `Find the average unit price for each product.` | `df.groupby('Product')['UnitPrice'].mean().reset_index()` |
| **5** | Advanced | `Show the top 3 customers who spent the most total revenue.` | `df.groupby('CustomerName')['TotalRevenue'].sum().nlargest(3).reset_index()` |

## 6. Excel Spreadsheets (.xlsx — Excel Formula Mode)

*Note: In Excel mode, OmniData AI produces copyable modern Excel formulas with range logic and evaluates the preview in the Data Table.*

### 6.1 `samples/6_excel_spreadsheets/single/financial_budget.xlsx`

| **5** | Advanced | `Give me an Excel formula to check if actual expense exceeded the allocated budget.` | `=IF(ExpenseDetails!D2 > DepartmentBudget!C2, "Over Budget", "Within Budget")` |
